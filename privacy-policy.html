Nexus Talent Scanner

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This policy explains how Nexus Talent Scanner (the “App”) handles information when you use it. It applies to the Android application identified by package name com.nexus.talentscanner.

The App does not require an account. Search keywords, filters, preferences, downloaded job listings, and saved jobs are stored on your device. The App also uses Google Firebase Analytics for app usage measurement.

Information stored on your device

The App stores information locally to provide its features, including:

  • Include and exclude keywords you choose, and your selected filters;
  • Saved job listings and their public listing details;
  • Downloaded public job-listing data and a dataset sync version marker;
  • App preferences such as theme and sync metadata; and
  • Small values used by the optional home-screen widget, such as new and seen job counts.

This information is kept in the App’s local storage and is not sent to Nexus as a user profile. It is generally removed when you clear the App’s storage or uninstall the App. Saved jobs and preferences are not necessarily included in your device backup; backup behavior depends on your Android settings.

Information processed by Google Firebase Analytics

The App uses Firebase Analytics, a service provided by Google, to understand aggregate app usage and improve the App. The App enables analytics collection. Analytics may process app-open and screen-view events, stage navigation, and, when those features or events are used, limited activity details such as scan outcomes, counts, or job and company hints.

Firebase and Google may also automatically process app-instance or device identifiers, device and app information, operating-system information, usage and diagnostic data, and network information such as IP address or a coarse region inferred from it, as described in Google’s Privacy Policy and Firebase privacy and security information. Google processes this information to provide analytics to the App developer and under its own service terms. Nexus does not use this information to create an account profile or to serve advertising.

Information sent to other services

  • Job data providers: The App requests public configuration and job-dataset files from hosting providers. The configured sources currently include GitHub and Hugging Face. These services receive ordinary request information, such as your IP address and request metadata, when your device downloads data. The providers process requests under their own privacy policies.
  • Company websites: If you choose to open a job’s application link, the link opens the relevant external website. That website may collect information under its own privacy policy. Nexus does not control those websites.
  • Google Play and Android: App installation, distribution, crash reporting, and notification permission are subject to Google Play, Android, and your device settings. Job and update notifications are generated by the App; the App does not send your saved-job list to a notification provider.

The App does not sell personal information. It does not provide Nexus with your contacts, photos, precise location, or account credentials. The App does not require you to submit a resume or create an account.

Why information is used

Local information is used to filter, display, and save job listings, remember your preferences, and show counts in the optional home-screen widget. Network requests retrieve public job data and check for dataset or app updates. Analytics information is used to measure usage and maintain and improve the App.

Retention and your choices

  • You can remove saved jobs and keywords in the App, or clear the App’s storage or uninstall it to remove locally stored App data.
  • You can disable notifications or restrict background activity in Android settings. Background checks may use network access to check whether public job data or app updates are available.
  • Firebase Analytics retains analytics data according to the applicable Google Analytics settings and policies. You can review Google’s privacy controls and your Android advertising/privacy settings. Disabling device-level advertising personalization does not necessarily disable Analytics measurement.

Children’s privacy

The App is intended for people seeking employment opportunities and is not designed for or directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security and changes

Data stored by the App remains on your device and is protected by the security features of your operating system. Information transmitted to third-party services is subject to their security practices. No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

This policy may be updated when the App’s features or data practices change. The “Last updated” date above indicates when the current version was prepared. Material changes will be reflected on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the developer at nexusearchjob@gmail.com.
